# My Favorite Series: *Chiikawa*

## Overview
Chiikawa (short for *Nanka Chiisakute Kawaii Yatsu*, or "Something Small and Cute") is a Japanese series created by **Nagano**. It started as a manga posted on Twitter in 2020 and later got an anime adaptation by *Doga Kobo* in 2022.

> It looks cute, but it can be surprisingly scary and sad sometimes.

---

## Why I Like It
- The characters are super cute and easy to love
- Each episode is short, so it's easy to watch
- It mixes wholesome moments with dark surprises

## Main Characters
| Character | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Chiikawa | Shy and cries easily, but works hard |
| Hachiware | Cheerful cat who's always positive and plays guitar |
| Usagi | Loud, chaotic rabbit-like creature who yells "Ura!" |
| Momonga | Spoiled flying squirrel who wants attention |
| Kurimanju | Laid-back character who loves food and drinks |

## What They Do in the Series
1. Pull weeds to earn money
2. Fight monsters
3. Take exams to get licenses
4. Eat good food together
